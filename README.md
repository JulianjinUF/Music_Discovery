# Music Discovery
 
A website for searching artists and songs, saving favorites, and building playlists.
This is our final project for CPSC 349 (Web Front-End Application Development).
 
**Team members**
- Julian Wang
- Shigeyasu Kameda
## About the project
 
Music Discovery gives people one simple place to look up music instead of searching through a bunch of different websites. You can search for a song or an artist and see details like the song title, artist name, album, and genre. You can also save songs you like, favorite them, and put them into playlists.
 
The site is mainly for people who enjoy listening to music and want an easy way to find and keep track of their favorite songs.
 
## Features
 
- Search bar for songs and artists
- Favorites
- Playlist creation
- Search history
- Listening history
## Pages
 
| Page | File | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home / Search | `index.html` | Search for music and see the results |
| Playlists / Favorites | `playlists.html` | View favorites and create playlists |
| History | `history.html` | See past searches and listening history |
